Privacy Policy – My Web Hub

Last updated: 7 November 2026

My Web Hub (the website mywebhubsite.com and the My Web Hub app) is a simple launcher that lets you save and open your favourite websites. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising trackers in My Web Hub.

Your saved websites

The list of websites you add is saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or your browser data deletes this list.

Website icons

To show each website's icon, the app asks Google's public favicon service for the icon of that website's domain (for example "youtube.com"). Google may receive that domain name and your device's IP address, as with any normal web request. Google's handling of this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Websites you open

When you tap a website, it opens that site in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy, and My Web Hub is not responsible for it.

Offline use

The app keeps a copy of its own pages on your device so it can open without internet. This copy contains no personal information.

Children

My Web Hub does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact email shown on the My Web Hub page on Google Play.
